## About you and fit
Tell us a bit about yourself and your research. Keep it interesting.
Can you describe your identity as a researcher, and how you arrived at your streams of research?
Which research conversation would you say you belong to?
Why did you go into academia?
Why are you interested in this role?
Why this school? What is it about us that interests you?
How do you see yourself contributing to our department?
How do you see yourself contributing to the mission of our school?
Why do you want to live in this city or region?
What would stop you from joining our school?
What other schools are you applying to?
What are your top three schools, not including us? What do those three have in common with us?
On a scale from 1 to 10, what is your desire to come to our school?
What is your ideal department? What qualities do you look for when choosing where to work?
What do you dislike about our department?
Why should we NOT hire you?
Tell us what you need to be successful here.
What was the hardest part of the job market for you, and what did it teach you?
Which professional association division or subgroup (for example, within the Academy of Management) do you fit into best?
How did you get interested in the topic you are studying?
Why is your work a fit for a business school (or this kind of department) rather than a disciplinary department?
How would you describe your work to a scholar outside your subfield?
